Reading The Incumbents

What They Get Right
  • Recurring revenue is real. $8M to $30M ARR at HR for Health and $6M to $15M at CEDR. Willingness to pay is proven.
  • Vertical focus matters. Both stayed in healthcare. Generic HR vendors do not win in this market.
  • Compliance is the wedge. HIPAA, OSHA, and HR overlap is the buyer's loudest pain point.
Where They're Vulnerable
  • Service-heavy, not product-heavy. Margins capped, scaling hard.
  • No AI, no automation. Built before the productivity reset.
  • No CRM or operations integration. Point tools, not operating systems.
  • Weak mobile, weak permissions. Modern owners notice immediately.
  • No movement, no founder. They sell software. We sell rebellion and empower independent dentistry and healthcare.

Investor Takeaway

  • The market is proven. $3B+ TAM. HR for Health at $40M to $150M valuation. CEDR at $15M to $60M. Two incumbents prove independent practices will pay recurring SaaS dollars for healthcare HR and compliance.
  • The wedge is real. Both incumbents are HR-only or compliance-consulting only. Neither has built a unified operating system. The gap between point tool and OS is ours to take.
  • Pricing inside the incumbent band. At $300/mo, MedRebel HR sits inside the $149 to $500 range buyers already pay. The HR app is the entry point. Once a practice is in, every add-on module compounds the switching cost.
  • Capital efficient by design. Built in 81 days with $0 outside funding and $0 equity given up. 3,700 effective senior dev hours, AI-assisted. Every dollar of future ARR converts to clean enterprise value.
  • Exit math is straightforward. At 4x to 10x revenue multiples (the healthcare HR SaaS comp band), $5M ARR maps to $20M to $50M enterprise value. $10M ARR maps to $40M to $100M. The incumbents got there. We are built to get there faster.
